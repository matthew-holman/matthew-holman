# 👋 Hi, I’m Matthew Holman

**Engineering & Product Leader** with 7+ years building and scaling remote-first teams.  
I take a pragmatic approach, adapting my leadership to each team’s context. I bridge product and engineering when needed and focus on creating the conditions for engineers to do their best work — bringing clarity and momentum to complex projects.

---

## 🧭 Leadership Profile

- 🤝 **People-first:** Coaching, clear expectations, and trust as the foundation for high-performing teams.  
- 🚀 **Delivery-focused:** Roadmaps into actionable slices, retros with actions that stick, and tech debt discussions that translate into real improvements
- 🗺️ **Product-minded:** Comfortable owning product strategy when needed, aligning stakeholders, and driving outcomes.
- 🔄 **Pragmatic agilist:** I believe in agile principles but not dogmatism — working iteratively, learning empirically, and adapting practices to the team’s context, challenges, and strengths.
- 📊 **Data-informed:** Measure for purpose, with intent — metrics that bring clarity and drive positive change.
- 🌍 **Remote-Leadership:** Experienced with diverse, distributed EMEA teams — fostering inclusion, knowledge sharing, and sustainable ways of working.

## 🌱 What drives me
I’m driven by solving teams’ problems, helping engineers grow, and building things that make a positive impact. I also focus on improving the working environment for teams, enabling them to discover better ways to build software and enjoy the process of doing it.
